I wrenched the wheel, turning off the highway onto a desolate gravel road. The sign was rusted, a skeleton of metal barely visible in the sweep of my headlights: Clearwater Psychiatric Facility. It wasn't a hospital. It was a warehouse for broken things, a dumping ground for the Family's liabilities. We used it for people who knew too much to live freely, but couldn't be killed for political reasons. Or for blood relatives who had lost their minds. It was a place where people checked in and never checked out.Ilene saw the sign. The crying stopped instantly. Then, the screaming began."No.No. You can't put me in there.I'm not crazy. " "You orchestrated a kidnapping to destroy a marriage," I said, my voice devoid of emotion. "You hallucinated threats where there were none. You are a danger to yourself and everyone around you. " I pulled up to the gate. The guards recognized my vehicle immediately. They opened the heavy iron gates without a word. I drove up to the main entrance where two orderlies were waiting. They were massive men.Silent men. I unlocked the doors."Get out. " "No. " Ilene clung to the leather upholstery."Ethan, please.I'm sorry. I'll apologize to her.I'll do anything. " I didn't wait.I exited the vehicle and strode to her door, ripping it open. I grabbed her arm and hauled her out. She went limp, becoming dead weight as I dragged her, her heels scraping uselessly through the gravel. The orderlies stepped forward and took her from me.One on each arm. "Mr. Bruce," one acknowledged with a curt nod. "She is to have no visitors," I commanded."No phone calls.No mail. As far as the world is concerned, she doesn't exist. " "Understood. " "Ethan. " Ilene shrieked. She twisted in their iron grip, reaching for me. Her eyes were wide, filled with a primal terror that finally mirrored what Aurora must have felt."You owe me." she screamed. "My father saved your life. " I met her gaze, my eyes cold. "He saved my life," I said. "And I just spared yours.The debt is paid. " I turned my back on her."Ethan. Don't leave me here.It's dark. I'm afraid of the dark. " Her voice cracked, dissolving into a raw, animalistic sound. I returned to the car. I slid into the driver's seat.I slammed the door. The heavy glass and steel instantly severed the sound of her screaming. I watched through the rearview mirror as they dragged her through the double doors. The doors swung shut.She was gone. I sat in the silence for a long time. My hands were still tacky with Leo's blood. My shirt was ruined. I had destroyed my enemies. I had avenged the insult. I had cleaned house.I was the victor.
